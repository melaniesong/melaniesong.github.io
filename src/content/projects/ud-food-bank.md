## Overview

The University District Food Bank has supported families across Northeast Seattle for more than forty years. As an organization focused on community care, food access, and dignity, its digital presence plays an important role in helping visitors quickly find services, volunteer opportunities, and ways to support the organization.

This project focused on creating a cohesive visual identity and website experience that reflects the food bank's mission while making essential information easier to access.

</section>

<section id="challenge">

## The Challenge

Food banks serve a wide range of audiences, each with different goals and needs.

Families seeking food assistance often need immediate answers about eligibility, locations, and operating hours. Volunteers want to understand how they can get involved. Donors need confidence that their contributions are making an impact.

The existing experience lacked a unified visual language and clear content hierarchy, making it difficult to communicate information consistently across pages.

The challenge was to create a system that could support multiple audiences while remaining approachable, trustworthy, and easy to navigate.

## Design System

### Creating Visual Hierarchy Through Color

The color palette was intentionally limited to five core colors to create consistency and reduce visual noise.

Terracotta was reserved for primary actions such as "Get Food Today" and "Donate" to ensure important actions remained highly visible.

Olive green became the primary structural color, reinforcing the organization's connection to food, community, and growth.

![Brand Values](/projects/ud-food-bank/palette.png)

All color combinations were evaluated against accessibility guidelines to maintain readability across the site.

![Brand Values](/projects/ud-food-bank/accessibility.png)


### Balancing Clarity and Warmth

The typography system combines Kind Sans and Lora.

Kind Sans provides structure, readability, and clear hierarchy across navigation, headings, and body copy.

Lora introduces warmth and personality through taglines, pull quotes, and moments of emphasis.

Together, they create a visual tone that feels trustworthy, welcoming, and approachable.

### Reimagining the Food Bank Identity

The logo combines two mirrored thumbprints that form the silhouette of an apple.

![Logo concept](/projects/ud-food-bank/logo-equation.png)

The fingerprint represents human connection and care, while the apple references the organization's mission around food access.

The result is a mark that feels personal, recognizable, and closely tied to the community it serves.

![Logo concept](/projects/ud-food-bank/logo-app.png)


### Designing for Consistency at Scale

Rather than designing individual pages independently, I created a responsive layout system that could support future growth.

The system establishes consistent hierarchy, spacing, and content flow across desktop and mobile experiences. This foundation makes it easier to introduce new content while maintaining a cohesive experience.

![Layout](/projects/ud-food-bank/layout.png)

The homepage was structured around visitor priorities, guiding users from immediate needs such as food access and operating hours toward programs, community resources, and opportunities for engagement.

<section id="reflection">

## Reflection

This project pushed me to think beyond individual screens and focus on designing systems.

Creating the visual identity, layout framework, and documentation required balancing flexibility with consistency. Every decision needed to work not only for the pages being designed today, but also for future pages and communication materials that may not exist yet.

One of my biggest takeaways was the importance of establishing clear design principles early. Having a strong foundation made it easier to evaluate decisions and maintain consistency across the entire system.

If I continued this project, I would explore usability testing with food bank clients, volunteers, and donors to validate how effectively the system supports each audience's goals.
